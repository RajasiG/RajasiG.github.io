01 — About
Research should not end at the paper.

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, working across artificial intelligence, distributed computing, edge systems, and human-computer interaction.

I enjoy taking a problem from the messy real world, understanding the data and people involved, designing the computational system, and eventually putting that system on a device, platform, or workflow where it can make a difference.

My projects have included AI assistants for programming education, machine learning for unobtrusive gait analysis, IoT systems for smart public infrastructure, and distributed data-processing systems.

04 — Research Map

Four questions guide my work.

01

How can AI help?

Designing useful generative-AI systems that support people without removing human reasoning from the process.

02

Where should AI run?

Exploring edge and distributed architectures that bring computation closer to data, devices, and users.

03

How do we build it?

Combining machine learning with robust data pipelines, APIs, containers, monitoring, and reproducible workflows.

04

Who benefits?

Keeping usability, human behavior, learning, and real-world constraints central to technical system design.
